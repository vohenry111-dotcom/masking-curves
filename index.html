Safety and calibration Use headphones in a quiet room. Start low, listen only at a comfortable level, and stop if any sound is uncomfortable. This app is uncalibrated: levels are dBFS, not dB SPL. Do not change the operating-system volume during a session.

04 · Reflect

AI prediction and engineering log

Hypothesis before measurement

Masking should be strongest at or near the masker frequency, decline with frequency separation, and may spread asymmetrically. Curves may become broader in Hz at higher masker center frequencies. This is a qualitative prediction, not a measured result.

05 · Data controls

Save and export

The active session autosaves after every response in this browser’s local storage.

Manual end-to-end test checklist
  • Complete the headphone warning and start a session.
  • Play a trial; confirm both intervals have equal timing and only one has a probe.
  • Confirm response buttons and keys are locked until playback ends.
  • Submit correct and incorrect responses; observe 2-down, 1-up behavior.
  • Refresh the page and resume the session.
  • Export and re-import JSON; export both CSV files and PNG charts.
  • Run developer tests and inspect any failures before collecting data.
